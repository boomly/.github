# Support

## What this is

A collection of online marketing experiments, published mostly for the record.
Repositories are provided as-is, without warranty — see each repository's
license.

## Getting help

- **Questions and half-formed ideas** — Discussions, if enabled on the repo.
- **A specific bug or a failing experiment** — open an issue with the steps to
  reproduce and what you expected instead.
- **A security issue** — follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a
  public issue.

## What is not offered

- No commercial support, no SLAs, and no guaranteed response time.
- No help running your own campaigns or accounts.
- No access to private data, accounts, or credentials used in an experiment.

## Before you ask

Check the repository README and the closed issues — most experiments document
their setup, their result, and why they were abandoned.
